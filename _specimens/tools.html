Species Survey Calendar

Interactive tool · Biological Resources

Plan field work around the seasonal windows when protected species can be surveyed.

The Species Survey Calendar is an interactive reference that maps the recommended survey windows for protected species across the year. Rotate through the wheel to see when each species can be detected in the field, when restrictions apply, and how those windows overlap — so project teams can scope biological surveys with confidence and avoid costly schedule conflicts.

ESA biologists maintain the underlying species and seasonality data, drawing on regulatory guidance and decades of field experience. The calendar is designed to give project managers, planners, and agency partners a fast, visual answer to the question: “Can we survey for this species now?”
